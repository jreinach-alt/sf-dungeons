# SuperForge Dungeons — Enemy Sprite Art Guidance (owner brief, 2026-11-17)

Reference: a generated four-enemy sprite sheet (`enemy_sheet_reference.png`).
Target: 32×32 sprites, 3/4 top-down perspective, SNES action RPG. Use the sheet as visual guidance
for silhouettes, proportions, palettes and animation states. Preserve each creature's distinctive
anatomy and movement. Favour readability over fine detail.

## 1. Bellwretch — snail + hermit crab + haunted cathedral bell
- Appearance: squat creature under an oversized tarnished bronze bell. Pale skeletal crab legs and
  claws emerge underneath. One glowing amber eye peers from the darkness.
- Silhouette: heavy bell-shaped dome with protruding claws.
- Movement: slow, deliberate six-legged scuttle. The bell rocks and tilts with each step.
- Attack: plants its legs, rocks the bell violently, then releases an expanding circular sound shockwave.
- Cues: idle rocking → scuttling → exaggerated bell tilt → shockwave.

## 2. Thornhopper — tree frog + praying mantis + carnivorous flower
- Appearance: plump emerald amphibian with enormous folded mantis-like hind legs. Its head resembles
  a crimson flower bud with a single yellow eye at its centre.
- Silhouette: low green body, tall angular hind legs, prominent red flower head.
- Movement: short, springy hops between periods of stillness. Legs compress dramatically before jumping.
- Attack: crouches low, opens its flower petals, then launches itself at the player in a fast leap.
- Cues: still crouch → leg compression → airborne extension → landing bounce.

## 3. Candlemaw — anglerfish + tortoise + melting candle
- Appearance: hunched, dark quadruped under a thick mound of dripping ivory wax. A brilliant blue flame
  floats above its back. Its enormous horizontal mouth holds four prominent teeth.
- Silhouette: rounded wax mound, blue flame, enormous black mouth, four stubby legs.
- Movement: slow, uneven waddling. The wax body sways while the flame flickers independently.
- Attack: the flame briefly extinguishes as a warning. Its mouth opens impossibly wide before a sudden
  forward lunge.
- Cues: flame flicker → flame extinguishes → mouth opens → lunge and bite.

## 4. Crownling — stag beetle + jellyfish + broken porcelain crown
- Appearance: a floating ivory crown-shaped shell with five uneven, cracked points. One oversized violet
  eye hangs beneath the shell, surrounded by four thick purple tendrils.
- Silhouette: asymmetric crown above a single eye, with dangling tentacles.
- Movement: gentle jellyfish-like hovering and drifting. Tendrils undulate. Rotates to track the player.
- Attack: stops drifting, retracts its tendrils, charges its glowing eye, then fires a narrow violet beam.
- Cues: hover bob → tendril contraction → eye brightens → beam discharge.

## Shared constraints
Consistent 3/4 top-down view with visible upper surfaces and clear facing. Recognisable silhouette at
native 32×32. About 3–4 dominant colours per creature, selective highlights and shadows. Strong key
poses over smoothness. Every attack has a distinct anticipation pose readable without particles.
Personality in the movement: Bellwretch is heavy, Thornhopper spring-loaded, Candlemaw lumbering,
Crownling weightless. Dark 1980s storybook fantasy, tactile organic materials; avoid generic low-poly
or modern cartoon looks. Sprites isolated: transparent background, no labels or neighbours.
Core objective: each creature recognisable from its silhouette alone, its movement communicating its
personality and attack behaviour.
